About Weather Pulse 🌦️

Weather Pulse is a professional weather application that provides live weather updates, forecasts, and useful tips for planning your day. Our mission is to give users a visually engaging, fast, and accurate weather experience using the OpenWeatherMap API.

The app is fully responsive and uses smooth animations, interactive UI elements, and multi-theme backgrounds to make weather tracking enjoyable.
